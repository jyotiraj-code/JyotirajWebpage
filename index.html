Doctoral researcher at Massey University (Auckland, New Zealand) within the Prof. Joachim Brand Research Group. Specializing in many-body quantum dynamics, non-equilibrium field theories, and stochastic quantum Monte Carlo propagators.

My computational focus centers on simulating non-linear solitonic dynamics in attractive Bose-Einstein condensates (BECs), real-time Full Configuration Interaction Quantum Monte Carlo (FCIQMC), and non-Hermitian extensions of the 1D Fermi-Hubbard lattice.
